Past predictions

Each match from the daily list, with the number the model gave before it started, the market's view and the best price (decimal or American, your choice). Once the result is in, you can see whether the model was right and how a flat 1-unit value bet would have done.

Summary

Predictions

“Model” and “Market” are each player's win chance (the market's with the bookmaker margin removed). The value bet is the side where the model's chance times the best price came out above 1, the same rule as the track record on the predictor.

Past parlays

Recommended parlays as they were posted, moved here once their first match starts (up to three live at a time on the predictor, for bookmakers and for Kalshi), settled from the results above. Profit is for a 1-unit stake.

Where this comes from. The daily updater writes every prediction to history/predictions.csv and every parlay to history/parlays.csv before the match starts, and settles them when the result arrives. Nothing is edited after the fact.
