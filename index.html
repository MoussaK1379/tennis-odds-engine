Recommended parlays

Built only from value picks with no warnings, each leg at least +3% EV and a 40%+ chance by the model, priced at one bookmaker that offers every leg. A parlay multiplies the bookmaker's margin and the model's mistakes, so treat these as small stakes at most. Up to three at a time: a parlay stays, unchanged, until its first match starts, then moves to Past predictions, and a new one from upcoming matches takes its place at the next refresh.

Kalshi parlays prediction market

The same rules as above, priced at Kalshi (after its trading fee), with their own three slots that roll the same way. Kalshi builds multi-leg bets ("combos") in its app, so each leg links to its Kalshi market; the ticket price here multiplies the legs' prices, and Kalshi's own combo quote may differ a little.
